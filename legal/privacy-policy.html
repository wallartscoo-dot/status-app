What we collect

Your name, username, email address, and content you upload or interact with (favorites, downloads, follows, uploaded videos/images). We collect basic usage analytics (screen views and key actions like publishing or downloading a status) tied to your account, used only to improve recommendations and the product — never sold to third parties.

How we use it

To run your account, show you relevant content, personalize recommendations, and improve the app. We do not sell personal data to third parties or use it for third-party advertising.

Content you upload

You retain ownership of anything you upload. By publishing, you grant Status App a license to host and display it in the app. See our Terms of Service for the copyright/DMCA process.

Data sharing

We use third-party infrastructure providers (hosting, database, object storage) solely to operate the service; they do not use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Data retention & deletion

You can edit or delete your profile and content at any time in the app. Contact privacy@statusapp.example.com to request full account deletion, including analytics history.

Children's privacy

Status App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Notification preferences are controlled in Settings within the app. You can browse as a guest without an account, with reduced functionality (no favorites, uploads, or personalized recommendations).

Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date above when this policy changes materially, and notify active users via an in-app notification for significant changes.
